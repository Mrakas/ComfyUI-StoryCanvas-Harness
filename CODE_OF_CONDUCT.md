# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility, apologize to those affected by mistakes, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, unwanted sexual attention, or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others’ private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberate credential exposure, cost abuse, or misuse of generated media.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers through the repository’s security/contact mechanisms. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the reporter’s privacy and safety.
